## Overview

Begun under Emperor Vespasian around AD 70–72 and inaugurated under his son Titus in AD 80, the Colosseum (formally the Flavian Amphitheatre) staged gladiatorial contests, animal hunts and public spectacles for crowds of tens of thousands.

## Quick facts

- **Built:** c. AD 70–80
- **Structure:** Concrete, travertine and tuff, arranged in three tiers of arches
- **Capacity:** Estimated at 50,000 spectators or more
- **Hypogeum:** A network of tunnels and chambers beneath the arena floor for animals, scenery and fighters

## Visiting

Combined tickets usually cover the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill next door, which makes for a natural full-day itinerary.
