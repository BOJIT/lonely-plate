## Overview

Machu Picchu was built around the mid-1400s, most likely as a royal estate for the Inca emperor Pachacuti. It was abandoned about a century later, around the time of the Spanish conquest, and the surrounding forest slowly reclaimed it. It became widely known internationally after Hiram Bingham's 1911 expedition.

## Quick facts

- **Elevation:** About 2,430 metres above sea level
- **Construction:** Dry-stone masonry cut so precisely that mortar was unnecessary
- **Highlights:** The Temple of the Sun, the Intihuatana stone and agricultural terraces
- **Heritage:** UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1983

## Getting there

Most visitors take the train from the Cusco area to Aguas Calientes, then a short bus ride up the mountain. Entry is by timed ticket.
