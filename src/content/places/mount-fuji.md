## Overview

Mount Fuji rises to 3,776 metres on the island of Honshu, straddling Shizuoka and Yamanashi prefectures. It is an active stratovolcano, though its last eruption was in 1707–1708. For centuries it has been a pilgrimage destination and a subject for artists, most famously Hokusai.

## Quick facts

- **Height:** 3,776 metres
- **Type:** Stratovolcano
- **Climbing season:** Roughly early July to early September
- **Heritage:** UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2013, recognised as a cultural landscape

## Visiting

The five lakes area on the north side offers some of the best views of the cone, especially in autumn and winter when the air is clearest.
