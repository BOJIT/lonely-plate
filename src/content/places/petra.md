## Overview

Petra flourished from roughly the 4th century BC as a hub on incense and spice trade routes. Its builders carved temples, tombs and monuments directly into rose-coloured sandstone cliffs, and engineered an ingenious system of dams and channels to supply water in the desert.

## Quick facts

- **Builders:** The Nabataean Arabs
- **Famous façade:** Al-Khazneh, the "Treasury", revealed at the end of the Siq
- **Heritage:** UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1985
- **Walking:** The site is large, and the climb to the Monastery involves hundreds of steps

## Visiting

Allow a full day, wear sturdy shoes, and bring plenty of water. Petra by Night offers a candlelit walk through the Siq on selected evenings.
