## Overview

Danish architect Jørn Utzon won an international design competition in 1957 with a concept of overlapping shell-like roofs rising above the harbour. The building took until 1973 to complete, after years of engineering challenges, and was opened by Queen Elizabeth II.

## Quick facts

- **Architect:** Jørn Utzon
- **Opened:** 1973
- **Roof:** Covered in more than a million glossy and matte white tiles
- **Heritage:** UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2007

## Visiting

Guided tours run daily, and an evening performance is the best way to see the interiors. The harbour walk from the Royal Botanic Garden gives classic views.
