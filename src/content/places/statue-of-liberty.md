## Overview

*Liberty Enlightening the World* was designed by sculptor Frédéric Auguste Bartholdi, with the internal iron framework engineered by Gustave Eiffel. Dedicated in 1886, it was a gift from the people of France marking friendship between the two nations.

## Quick facts

- **Material:** Copper sheets over an iron framework, now green from natural patina
- **Dedicated:** 28 October 1886
- **Location:** Liberty Island, reached by ferry
- **Heritage:** UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1984

## Visiting

Ferries depart from Battery Park in Manhattan and Liberty State Park in New Jersey. Crown access is limited and needs advance reservation.
