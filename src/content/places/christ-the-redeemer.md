## Overview

Completed in 1931, the statue was designed by Heitor da Silva Costa with sculptor Paul Landowski and is made of reinforced concrete clad in thousands of small soapstone tiles. It looks out over Guanabara Bay and the city from the summit of Corcovado.

## Quick facts

- **Height:** About 30 metres, plus an 8-metre pedestal
- **Arm span:** Roughly 28 metres
- **Style:** Art Deco
- **Recognition:** Named one of the New Seven Wonders of the World in 2007

## Visiting

The cog railway from Cosme Velho climbs through Tijuca Forest and is the classic way up. Go on a clear morning, as cloud often covers the summit.
