## Overview

The Great Pyramid was built for Khufu of Egypt's Fourth Dynasty, around 2560 BC. For several thousand years it was the tallest human-made structure on Earth, and it is the only one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World still largely standing.

## Quick facts

- **Original height:** About 146 metres, slightly less today after losing its capstone and outer casing
- **Material:** Millions of limestone blocks, with a smooth casing of finer limestone once covering the sides
- **Neighbours:** The pyramids of Khafre and Menkaure, and the Great Sphinx

## Visiting

Arrive early to beat the heat and the crowds. Entering the pyramid itself needs a separate ticket and is not suited to anyone who dislikes confined spaces.
