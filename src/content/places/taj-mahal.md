## Overview

Construction began in 1632 after the death of Mumtaz Mahal and the main mausoleum was largely complete by around 1643, with the wider complex of gardens, mosque and gateway finished about a decade later. It stands on the south bank of the Yamuna River.

## Quick facts

- **Style:** Mughal architecture, blending Persian, Islamic and Indian influences
- **Material:** White Makrana marble inlaid with semi-precious stones
- **Layout:** A symmetrical *charbagh* garden, with the tomb at one end
- **Heritage:** UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1983

## Visiting

The marble shifts in colour through the day, so early morning is popular. The monument is closed to the general public on Fridays.
