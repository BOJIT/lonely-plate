## Overview

The Eiffel Tower was raised between 1887 and 1889 as the entrance arch to the Exposition Universelle, the world's fair marking the centenary of the French Revolution. It was meant to be temporary, but its usefulness as a radio antenna saved it from demolition.

## Quick facts

- **Engineer:** Gustave Eiffel's company, with structural design by Maurice Koechlin and Émile Nouguier
- **Material:** Puddled iron, in an open lattice that lets wind pass through
- **Height:** Roughly 330 metres today, antennas included
- **Visitors:** Among the most visited paid monuments in the world

> Once derided by artists and writers of the day, it became the symbol of the city it overlooks.

## Visiting

Book timed tickets ahead of time, and consider going near sunset: the tower sparkles for a few minutes at the top of each hour after dark.
